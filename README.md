### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Computer Science undergraduate focused on the intersection of Full-Stack Web Development (MERN) and Artificial Intelligence. 

Instead of building standard portfolio projects, I prioritize enterprise-grade architecture and security. My engineering philosophy revolves around building scalable systems that require real-world logic, from complex database aggregations to secure authentication pipelines. Over the past year, I have engineered immutable financial ledgers, timezone-aware cron scheduling systems, and role-based publishing applications utilizing soft-deletion architectures and HTTP-only JWTs to mitigate XSS vulnerabilities.

Alongside my independent architecture, I have completed three technical internships, ranging from developing Python algorithms to optimizing computer vision models for hazard detection.

**Open To:** Full-Stack Engineering, Backend Development, and Machine Learning roles where I can tackle complex architectural challenges and contribute to scalable products.
